# Visit workspace direction contract

THESIS: One consultation, one place. A short patient overview leads to a visit; prescriptions, reports, notes and recordings belong inside that visit rather than competing on the overview.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Geist, self-hosted Bengali type, zinc light/dark themes and the supplied pixel illustrations. Pixel category marks identify tasks; conventional forms carry clinical content. Restrained blue selection, amber review warnings, clear 44px controls.

STORY: The doctor chooses a patient, reads the concise health context, starts/resumes a visit, adds evidence and publishes corrections, then completes it. Patients open dated visits and their published contents. Consent stays patient controlled.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 208px desktop navigation rail and one content column. A single 28px patient/visit title, concise context, one primary next action and the first dated visit rows. On phones, compact header and four short navigation choices; the same content order without a stacked workspace banner or nested summary panels.

FORM: User-selected visit-centered workspace, code-led. The workflow and pixel identity were explicitly chosen; no visual concept lottery is needed for those settled choices. Signature interaction: enter a visit to work on one artifact, then return to the same consultation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
