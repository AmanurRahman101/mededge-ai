# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Facts below are taken from the approved MedEdge AI architecture plan. The interview step was skipped because that plan already answered the product questions and the build was instructed to continue without stopping. Nothing here is a visual decision.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: FastAPI backend and a Vite + React + TypeScript frontend, as specified in the plan. Backend deploys to Render, frontend to Vercel.

## Users

Two roles, and the first screen offers only these two.

- A patient who has a paper lab report or a handwritten prescription and wants it turned into a record they can look at and share.
- A doctor who opens that share, or uploads a report, dictates an administrative visit note, checks the result, and exports it.

## Product Purpose

Turn a paper lab report, a handwritten prescription, or a spoken visit note into a verified HL7 FHIR R4 bundle. Success is a bundle a doctor can approve, with every value still matching the page.

## Positioning

The page is hidden before any language model sees it. The patient confirms the redaction overlay. The bundle is checked by a verifier, and a failed check is repaired or labeled as assembled by fallback.

## Operating Context

A hackathon demo and a small clinical-admin workflow. Patients photograph reports at home. Doctors review on a laptop. Sample sheets in the repo are fictional drawings until a real, consented, redacted page is added by hand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Patients: add a report, confirm what is hidden, see values exactly as printed, share a 6-character code.
- Doctors: open a code or upload a report, see the source, the live trace, and the result, dictate a visit note, approve, and export.
- Prescriptions are copied as written. The app does not advise a dose, check interactions, or extract a diagnosis.
- The app does not compute high or low flags. It copies printed values and ranges only.
- Required line, on every screen: "MedEdge AI is an administrative data interoperability and optical ingestion pipeline, not a clinical decision or diagnostic tool."
- Raw photographs stay in gitignored `data/private/`. Nothing reaches a language model until the redaction is confirmed.
- The Patient resource carries a session pseudonym only.

## Brand Commitments

The name is MedEdge AI. Voice is plain and administrative. No diagnosis language, no reassurance about what a result means.

## Evidence on Hand

Fictional sample sheets in `data/samples/`, generated by `scripts/build_samples.py`. They are not photographs of real patients. Real reports are not in the repo. No testimonials.

## Product Principles

- One primary action per step.
- Show the printed value, not an interpretation.
- Say which model actually answered, and say when a result was assembled without a model.
- Hide identifiers before any language model sees the page.
- A failed check is visible. It is never a silent correction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must be usable with a keyboard, respect reduced motion, and keep text at or above a 4.5:1 contrast ratio. Dictation accepts Bengali and English.
